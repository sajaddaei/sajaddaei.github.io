research

Mathematical foundations, signal processing, wireless communications, and quantum sensing.

Research vision

From mathematical foundations to intelligent sensing and communications

My research develops principled methods for extracting useful information from incomplete, noisy and structured measurements. I connect applied mathematics, statistical signal processing and optimization with sensing, wireless communications, privacy-aware inference and emerging quantum technologies.

Research directions

Nine connected themes, from the mathematics of inverse problems to future communication and sensing systems. The illustrations are conceptual schematics, not experimental results.

01 / RESEARCH THEME

Compressed sensing & mathematical optimization

What is the minimum information needed for reliable recovery?

I study recovery guarantees, sample complexity and phase transitions for structured inverse problems, including weighted sparse and block-sparse recovery, total variation and dictionary-sparse models.

Representative publications

  • On the Error in Phase Transition Computations for Compressed Sensing (2019)
  • Measuring Less, Recovering More: Distribution-Aware Weighted ℓ₁ Analysis (2026)

02 / RESEARCH THEME

Low-rank recovery & computational imaging

How can missing or blurred information be reconstructed?

I develop prior-informed matrix sensing and completion methods, weighted nuclear-norm regularization and structured low-rank models for reconstructing signals and images from incomplete observations.

Representative publications

  • Multi-weight nuclear norm minimization for low-rank matrix recovery in presence of subspace prior information (2022)
  • Reconstruction of Binary Shapes from Blurred Images via Hankel-structured Low-rank Matrix Recovery (2019)

03 / RESEARCH THEME

Continuous-domain super-resolution & blind inference

How can continuous parameters be recovered without a discretization grid?

My work investigates atomic-norm methods, convex relaxation, blind deconvolution, demixing and multidimensional spectral estimation, including recovery from multiple measurement vectors and separation of radar signals from impulsive noise.

Representative publications

  • Timely and painless breakups: Off-the-grid blind message recovery and users’ demixing (2025)
  • Two-dimensional super-resolution via convex relaxation (2019)

04 / RESEARCH THEME

Near-field sensing, XL-MIMO & ISAC

How can we sense and communicate using large antenna apertures?

I develop continuous range-angle estimation and lifted super-resolution techniques for spherical-wave propagation, near-/far-field boundaries and collaborative integrated sensing and communications (ISAC).

Representative publications

  • Living Off the Grid: Continuous Range-Angle Super-Resolution for Near-Field XL-MIMO (2026)
  • A Mathematical Theory of Near-Field Super-Resolution (2026)
  • One target, many views: Multi-user fusion for collaborative uplink ISAC (2025)

05 / RESEARCH THEME

Massive MIMO & channel dynamics

How can wireless systems adapt to evolving channels?

I investigate spatial and temporal channel correlations, non-stationary Rician aging channels, channel estimation, pilot spacing, transmitter design and power allocation for multi-antenna communications.

Representative publications

  • Exploiting Spatial and Temporal Correlations in Massive MIMO Systems Operating Over Non-Stationary Aging Channels (2025)
  • Toward Optimal Pilot Spacing and Power Control in Multi-Antenna Systems Operating Over Non-Stationary Rician Aging Channels (2025)

06 / RESEARCH THEME

Massive access & goal-oriented detection

Can a network make the right decision without decoding everything?

I explore blind and asynchronous activity detection, random access, joint channel estimation and task-oriented inference for large populations of connected devices, including stationary IoT applications.

Representative publications

  • Blind goal-oriented massive access for future wireless networks (2023)
  • Enabling Massive Connectivity of Stationary IoT Devices via 2D Blind Goal-Oriented Detection (2025)

07 / RESEARCH THEME

Semantic communications & information privacy

What should be transmitted, and what should remain private?

My research examines task-relevant information transmission under privacy constraints, multi-task semantic communications, leakage bounds and privacy-preserving mechanism design.

Representative publications

  • Multi-task private semantic communication (2024)
  • Sparse Point-wise Privacy Leakage: Mechanism Design and Fundamental Limits (2026)

08 / RESEARCH THEME

Communication-efficient distributed computing

How can multiple agents compute collaboratively with less communication?

I study distributed computation using compressed sensing and matrix factorization, exploiting low-dimensional structure to reduce the information exchanged among computing nodes.

Representative publications

  • Multi-User Distributed Computing Via Compressed Sensing (2023)
  • Communication-Efficient Distributed Computing via Matrix Factorization (2024)

09 / RESEARCH THEME

Quantum signal processing & sensing

How can quantum representations support estimation and waveform design?

My emerging research addresses quantum-assisted near-field angle-range spectrum estimation, subspace projection, operator uncertainty and cross-domain information in quantum signals.

Representative publications

  • Q-SPARSE: Quantum Subspace Projection for Near-Field Angle-Range Spectrum Estimation (2026)
  • Operator uncertainty and cross-domain information in quantum signals: Second-moment bounds for quantum waveform design (2026)
