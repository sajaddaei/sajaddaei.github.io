<p class="sd-eyebrow">Research vision</p>
<h2>From mathematical foundations to intelligent sensing and communications</h2>
<p>My research develops principled methods for extracting useful information from incomplete, noisy and structured measurements. I connect applied mathematics, statistical signal processing and optimization with sensing, wireless communications, privacy-aware inference and emerging quantum technologies.</p>
