## Research Directions

- Mathematical and statistical signal processing
- Inverse problems and compressed sensing
- Continuous-domain super-resolution
- Optimization and mathematical foundations of AI
- Near-field sensing and wireless communications
- Quantum-assisted signal processing

## Funded Research Projects
