I am a researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology
in Stockholm, Sweden.

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of
signal processing, inverse problems, optimization,
and intelligent wireless systems.

## Research Interests

- Mathematical and statistical signal processing
- Inverse problems and compressed sensing
- Super-resolution and continuous-domain estimation
- Near-field sensing and communications
- Mathematical foundations of artificial intelligence

## Research Projects

- MULTIPLY-6G — KTH Co-Principal Investigator
- QUEST-6G — KTH Co-Principal Investigator and WP4 Leader

## Selected Awards

- IEEE SPAWC 2026 Best Paper Award
- IEEE GLOBECOM 2025 Best Proposal for Industry Research Collaboration
- IEEE Iran Section Outstanding Ph.D. Thesis Award, 2020
